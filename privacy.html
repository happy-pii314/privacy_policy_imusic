1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to iMusic - Offline Music Player ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed for Android devices. We are committed to protecting and respecting your personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information, the Android device permissions we request, and how third-party services integrated into the app function. By downloading, installing, or using iMusic, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Core Guarantee: iMusic is built primarily as an offline local media player. We believe that your personal music library belongs solely to you. We do not inspect, listen to, copy, or upload your audio files to any cloud or external infrastructure.

2. Android Device Permissions Requested

To deliver music playback, playlist management, and background controls, iMusic requests specific system permissions. Each permission is strictly limited to its stated purpose:

Permission Target Version Purpose & Justification
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Android 13+ (API 33+) Allows iMusic to discover, scan, and play local audio files (MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC) stored on your device storage. Does NOT access photos, videos, or documents.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 12 and below Allows legacy devices to read audio files from your external SD card or internal media directories.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Android 9+ / Android 14+ Enables seamless audio playback when the app is minimized or when the device screen is switched off, in accordance with Android background playback standards.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ (API 33+) Allows the app to show the persistent media notification containing playback controls (Play, Pause, Next, Previous, Song Title, and Album Artwork).
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 All Android versions Required strictly for third-party SDKs: serving Google AdMob advertisements and fetching dynamic configurations from Firebase Remote Config. The music engine itself does not stream audio or upload files.

3. Information Collection & Use

We classify information handling into two clear categories:

A. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Identity: We do not ask for your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or government IDs.
  • User Accounts: There is no user registration or sign-in mechanism; all features are accessible instantly without accounts.
  • Microphone or Recording: We never record your audio or capture ambient sound.
  • Financial Data: iMusic does not collect credit card, bank, or payment details.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

When you are connected to the Internet, third-party libraries (Google AdMob and Google Firebase) may automatically collect standard diagnostic and device data, including:

  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale/language).
  • Anonymous advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID), subject to your device settings.
  • Network connection state and coarse IP address (used for approximate geographic location to serve localized ads).
  • App performance metrics (crash logs, memory events, and ad impressions).

4. Local Storage & Audio Privacy

iMusic indexes your local audio files using the standard Android MediaStore API. Here is our solemn commitment regarding your media:

  • Zero Server Transmission: Your music files, audio tracks, artist metadata, album artwork, and personal voice notes never leave your smartphone.
  • Local Favorites & Preferences: When you mark a track as a favorite, customize playback order, or complete the onboarding tutorial, these settings are saved exclusively inside your device's private sandboxed storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Data Wipeout: If you uninstall iMusic or clear app data via Android Settings, all local preferences and favorite lists are permanently deleted from your phone.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

iMusic is offered as a free-to-use application. To support ongoing maintenance and feature development, we display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

AdMob may use cookies, device identifiers (AAID), and related technologies to serve relevant ads based on your interests or non-personalized generic ads.

Ad Formats displayed in iMusic:

  • App Open Ads: Brief display upon app launch.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen interstitial ads shown at measured intervals (e.g., when launching favorite tracks or navigating key screens).
  • Native & Banner Ads: Embedded seamlessly into navigation screens and the bottom banner.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services
  • Google Ads Personalization Controls

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or disable your Advertising ID at any time directly on your Android device: Navigate to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Firebase Remote Config & Diagnostics

iMusic integrates Google Firebase Remote Config to manage app configurations remotely. This allows us to adjust ad intervals, customize the onboarding screens, or enable bug fixes without requiring a mandatory Google Play app update.

  • Firebase Remote Config exchanges non-personal device attributes (such as app version, OS version, and language) to deliver the appropriate configuration payload.
  • No personal audio data, search queries, or user identities are transmitted to Firebase.
  • Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

7. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

We respect the privacy rights of users located across the globe, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), and California (USA).

European Union / UK Users (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Since we store no identifiable personal data on remote servers, your local music files and app preferences remain directly accessible on your device.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can erase all app-related preferences and cached data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may modify ad tracking permissions and notification permissions at any time within your device settings.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • You have the right to request disclosure of third-party advertising data practices.
  • You have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

iMusic does not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). Our offline audio player is intended for general audiences.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps to remove such information.

9. Data Security & Storage Integrity

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your experience:

  • No Cloud Storage: By avoiding external server uploads of your private media files, the risk of external database breaches or unauthorized audio leaks is virtually eliminated.
  • Sandboxed Android Storage: All favorites and configuration settings are stored inside the application's secure private directory, inaccessible to other standard Android applications.
  • Encrypted Transport: When communicating with Google AdMob and Firebase Remote Config, all network traffic is secured via encrypted HTTPS / TLS protocols.
